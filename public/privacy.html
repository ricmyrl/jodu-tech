Privacy policy

This policy describes how information is handled when you use the JODU-TECH website.

Information you enter

The consultation form asks for your name, email address, and project details. The website does not submit this information to a JODU-TECH server or store it in a website database. When you choose “Continue to WhatsApp,” the site opens WhatsApp with a message containing the details you entered. Review the message and send it yourself; if you do, WhatsApp and JODU-TECH receive and process it.

Third-party services

WhatsApp handles messages sent through its service under its own terms and privacy policy. The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts and some illustrative imagery from Unsplash; those services may receive standard connection data from your browser. The site does not currently use an advertising tracker or analytics tool.

Hosting and technical data

The hosting provider may process standard request and security logs to operate the website. The provider and retention period depend on where the site is deployed.

Your choice

You can use the website without completing the consultation form. Do not send sensitive personal, financial, or health information through the form or WhatsApp.
